                                                  Data storytelling
What is data storytelling?
  Crafting narratives from data
  Combining insights with visualizations
Purpose:
  Make complex data accessible
  Engage and inform your audience
Why it matters:
  Transforms raw data into insights
  Enhances decision-making

Before: showing data
  Cluttered line chart, difficult to interpret
  Raw data without context
After: storytelling
  Clear narrative highlighting key insights
  Simple bar chart emphasizing important data points

                                      The role of data visualizations in storytelling
Bar charts: compare quantities across categories
Line charts: show trends over time
Pie charts: display proportions and part-towhole relationships
Scatter plots: show relationships and correlations
Heat maps: reveal patterns and intensity of data
Histograms: show the distribution of numerical data
Area charts: highlight changes over time with stacked data

                                        Understanding statistical distributions
Why they matter
Reveal patterns in data
Highlight central tendencies: mean, median, mode
Show spread: range, variance, standard deviation
Identify shape: skewness, kurtosis
