                                    What is a dashboard?
Is a space for multiple visualizations
Displays related data together
Simplifies complex information
Helps compare metrics at a glance

Dashboards can:
  Identify trends quickly
  Track real-time data
  Make faster, informed decisions
  Support business strategy

                              Key components of a dashboard
Components:
Bar Charts: compare values
Line Charts: show trends
Maps: visualize locations
Counter Plots: show key performance
indicators (KPIs)
Filters and tools: customize views
